# App Shell & Navigation — Tooltip Help

Sibling of `docs/features/AppShell/spec.md`. Each section provides the Short variant (tooltip text, under 150 chars, shown on info icon hover).

---

## Sidebar Navigation

The persistent sidebar lets you switch between Dashboard, Developers, Sprints, Epics, and Settings.

---

## Dashboard

The landing page shown when you first open the app. Provides an overview of your team's sprint analytics.

---

## Theme Toggle

Switches the entire app between dark and light themes. Your choice is remembered across sessions.

---

## Empty States

Placeholder screens shown in sections with no data yet, explaining what to do next.

---

## Card-Based Layout

Analytics content is organized into cards — each metric or chart lives in its own visual container.

---

## Page Toolbar

The area at the top of analytics pages containing the sprint selector and sub-team filter.

---

## Sprint Selector

Choose a specific sprint to analyze, or select a range (Last 3, Last 5, All) for trend views.

---

## Sub-Team Filter

Restrict all page content to developers in a specific sub-team, or show everyone with "All."

---

## Design Tokens

Shared color definitions ensuring consistent appearance across all pages and features.

---

## Responsive Sidebar Collapse

The sidebar switches to icon-only mode automatically when your browser window is narrow.
