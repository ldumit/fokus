# Sprint Summary Card — Tooltip Help

Sibling of `docs/features/SprintSummaryCard/spec.md`. Each section provides the Short variant (tooltip text, under 150 chars, shown on info icon hover).

---

## Health Score

Weighted composite of completion, disruption, and carry-over rates. 0-100 scale with green/amber/red status.

---

## Health Score — Per-Metric Sub-Scores

Individual 0-100 scores for completion, disruption, and carry-over that feed the composite.

---

## SP Completed / Committed

Story points completed versus story points committed at sprint start.

---

## Completion %

Percentage of committed story points completed. Higher is better.

---

## Disruption Rate

Percentage of committed SP that was added mid-sprint. Lower is better.

---

## Carry-Over Rate

Percentage of total sprint scope (committed + added) not completed. Lower is better.

---

## Delta Indicator

Change versus the prior closed sprint. Arrow direction and color show whether the metric improved.

---

## Sparkline

Trend of the last 4 sprints ending at the selected sprint.

---

## Top Epics

Up to 3 epics with the most story points completed in this sprint.

---

## Developer Leaderboard

All active developers ranked by story points completed in this sprint.

---

## Zombie Tickets Flag

Tickets that have appeared in 3 or more sprints without being completed.

---

## Mid-Sprint Disruption Flag

Story points and ticket count added to the sprint after the first 2 days.

---

## Zero-SP Developers Flag

Active developers who had assigned tickets but completed zero story points.

---

## No Flags Message

All sprint health indicators passed with no issues detected.

---

## Sprint Selector

Choose which closed sprint to view. Most recent is selected by default.

---

## Sub-Team Filter

Restrict all dashboard content to developers in the selected sub-team.

---

## Empty State

No closed sprints available yet. Sync a completed sprint to see data.
