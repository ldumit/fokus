# Daily Developer Progress — Tooltips

## Daily Progress Tab
Shows each developer's daily ticket completion progress during the active sprint.

## Alert Banner
Developers whose completed SP is more than one day behind their expected pace.

## Burnup Chart
Cumulative story points completed (solid line) vs expected pace (dashed line) per day.

## Expected Pace Line
Straight diagonal based on assigned SP, capacity, and sprint length in calendar days.

## Behind-Pace Indicator
This developer's completed SP is more than one full day behind their expected pace.

## Stall Badge
Tickets in progress with no status change for 2+ business days (weekdays only).

## SP Completed / Assigned
Total story points completed out of total assigned (all types: features, bugs, tasks).

## Completion Percent
Percentage of assigned story points completed so far in this sprint.

## Day Drill-Down
Tickets completed on this day with their key, summary, story points, and type.

## Stalled Ticket — Days Since Last Transition
Business days (Mon-Fri) since this ticket's last status change in Jira.

## Grace Period
No behind-pace alerts during the first 2 days of the sprint while work ramps up.
